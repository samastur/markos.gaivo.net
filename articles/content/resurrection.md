Title: Resurrection
Date: 2026-10-23 19:34:42
Author: markos
Category: Catchall
Slug: resurrection
Tags: writing, blog

It has been almost five years since I last posted anything on my website. Not because I didn't want to or had nothing to write about; I just failed at it. All written communication became a difficulty I still suffer with and hence also from.

A lot has happened since my last post, a true roller coaster ride of experiences with a bleaker outlook now than back then, just before the renewed attack on Ukraine.

This year has been no less turbulent, with my industry going through a once-in-a-lifetime transformation, but I feel certain of a few things: I am reading books again, building things, and I will write more, even if in the meantime I have lost even those few readers I had.
